# fomotren

fomotren turns a workout program written in a chat into a daily plan on your Android phone. Your AI assistant helps write and revise the program. fomotren shows you what to do each day, guides you through workouts, and keeps your training history.

## How it works

1. **Make a plan.** Share the instructions from fomotren with ChatGPT, Claude, or another AI chat. Tell it your goals, equipment, and schedule. It returns a program as plain text in the app’s TRN 1 format. You can also open the built-in strength and mobility program to try the app right away.
2. **Load it and train.** Share the reply to fomotren, paste it, or open the program file. The app shows the scheduled workouts, sets, reps, and targets for your current week. Start a session for spoken cues and rest timers, or check off exercises yourself. You can also reschedule workouts, record training weights, and track measurements.
3. **Adjust the plan.** When you want feedback, share a two-week report from the Coach tab with your chat. The report includes what you completed, what you missed, measurements, and notes. Import the revised plan back into fomotren to keep training with it.

The app stores your program and training history on your phone. You choose what to send to the AI chat. English and Russian are available for the interface, built-in program, instructions, and spoken cues.

## Install

fomotren runs on Android 8.0 and later. Download the APK from [Releases](https://github.com/fomovoz/fomotren/releases/latest) and open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager.

If you already have a test build, save a backup from **Program → Backup** before updating. If Android reports a signature mismatch, uninstall the test build after saving your backup, install the release, and choose **Restore from backup** on the welcome screen.

Report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/fomovoz/fomotren/issues). Include the app version, your phone model, the Android version, and the steps that led to the problem.
